              How the game works

              • The agent reads one message at a time and decides whether to pay. It has no tools: it can only propose a payment. It is trusting on purpose, so fooling it is the easy part.
              • Sayso checks every proposed payment against the owner's signed rules. The wallet signs only on an allow.
              • You win if money leaves the pot for an address that is not one of our vendors. The transfer is yours (test ETH, no value). The game pauses after a win while we close the gap.
              • Real vendor invoices arrive on a timer, so allowed payments show in the feed too.
              • Attack the agent and the check only, not the server, the hosting or other players. Free to play. Full rules.
